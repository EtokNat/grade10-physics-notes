Grade 10 Physics · 10-minute Test · Weeks 5 & 6  |  Answer every question in your exercise book.  |  Time: 12 min  |  Use g = 10 m s⁻².
A · Definitions & Principles
  1. Define a fluid, and state the formula and SI unit of density.
  2. Define adhesion and cohesion.
  3. State the Law of Flotation and Archimedes' Principle.
  4. Define viscosity and terminal velocity.
  5. State Pascal's Principle.
B · Concept Understanding
  1. Explain, using cohesion and adhesion, why the meniscus of water in a glass tube is concave while that of mercury is convex.
  2. Give one everyday example of capillarity, and state one factor that reduces the surface tension of a liquid.
  3. State one factor that decreases the viscosity of a liquid, and one factor that increases the viscosity of a gas.
C · Light Calculations
  1. A stone weighs 6 N in air and 4 N when fully immersed in water. Calculate: (a) the upthrust on the stone; (b) the relative density of the stone.
  2. A liquid of density 800 kg m⁻³ fills a tank to a depth of 0.5 m. Calculate the pressure at the base of the tank.
Section A & B: one–two sentences each. Section C: show formula → substitution → answer with unit.   Total: 10 questions.
